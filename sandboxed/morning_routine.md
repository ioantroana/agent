# Morning Routine (about 60 minutes)

1. **Wake up at the same time every day** (6:30 AM). Keep a consistent wake time, even on weekends.
2. **Hydrate** (6:30–6:35). Drink a full glass of water before anything else.
3. **Move your body** (6:35–6:50). Do 10–15 minutes of stretching, a short walk, or a few bodyweight exercises like squats, push-ups, and planks.
4. **Refresh** (6:50–7:00). Shower, brush your teeth, and get dressed. Choose your clothes the night before to save decisions.
5. **Mindfulness** (7:00–7:10). Spend 5–10 minutes meditating, journaling, or writing down three things you're grateful for.
6. **Nourish** (7:10–7:25). Eat a breakfast with protein, fiber, and healthy fats, such as eggs with vegetables, Greek yogurt with berries, or oatmeal with nuts.
7. **Plan the day** (7:25–7:35). Review your calendar, pick your top three priorities, and decide what you'll tackle first.
8. **Disconnect, then connect** (7:35–7:45). Avoid your phone for the first 30 minutes after waking. Check messages and email only after this step.
9. **Leave on time** (7:45). Gather your bag, keys, and anything else you need, and head out.

## Tips
- Prepare as much as possible the night before (clothes, breakfast ingredients, bag).
- Start small. If the full routine feels like too much, begin with just water, movement, and planning, then add steps over time.
- Adjust the times to match your actual wake-up and work schedule.
